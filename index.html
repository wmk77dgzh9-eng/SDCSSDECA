What is DECA

A business club that actually competes.

DECA is an international organization for high school and college students interested in marketing, finance, hospitality, management and entrepreneurship. Members prepare for business case competitions, learn how to present to professionals, and build skills that carry into university applications and first jobs.

Ontario DECA has been running for over 47 years and is now the largest DECA association in the world. Thousands of Ontario students compete each year at regional, provincial and international conferences.

At St. David, the chapter meets to practise cases, run mock role plays, prepare for tests and get everyone ready to compete. You do not need to take a business course to join. Curiosity and a willingness to speak up are enough.

Joining

Four steps to become a founding member.

  1. Reach out to the chapter

    Email us or come to an info meeting so we can add you to our list and share updates.

  2. Register with Ontario DECA

    Fill out the student registration on the Ontario DECA website and choose your top three events. Our teacher advisor approves every application.

  3. Pay your membership fee

    The fee is paid to our teacher advisor at school, not online. Ontario DECA does not allow cancellations or refunds after October 15.

  4. Start training

    Come to meetings, practise cases with the team and get ready for Regionals in November.

Questions

Things people usually ask.

Do I need to be taking a business course?

No. Business courses help, but the chapter will teach you what you need. Principles events are designed for students who are new to DECA.

What actually happens in a role play?

You get a case you have never seen before. It describes a business problem and the role you are playing, such as a manager or consultant. You get a short prep period, then you meet a judge who plays the other person in the scenario and you present your ideas. Nobody sees the case ahead of time, so everyone starts even.

What do I wear to competitions?

Business attire. Think dress pants or a skirt, a collared shirt or blouse, and a blazer or tie if you have one. Bring photo ID to every competition, since Ontario DECA can take points off if you do not have it.

How much does it cost?

Membership is $35 USD per student, which covers your dues and the Regional competition. If you qualify for Provincials in Toronto there is an additional fee that includes a required hotel stay. Talk to us if cost is a concern.

I am in Grade 9. Is this for me?

Yes. DECA is open to all grades, and Provincials even runs a separate Leadership Academy just for Grade 9 and 10 students. Starting early gives you more seasons to improve.

How does this help after high school?

You practise public speaking, problem solving and professional skills that universities and employers look for. Results at Regionals and Provincials are also strong additions to applications for business programs.
